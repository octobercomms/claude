# Instagram ranking playbook

Condensed from Instagram's official "Ranking Explained" (Adam Mosseri). Instagram
has no single algorithm — each surface ranks by predicting which actions a viewer
will take, then ordering by the most heavily-weighted predicted actions. Design
content to maximise the actions each surface rewards.

## Feed (catch up with who you follow + suggested)
Top signals (≈ order): the viewer's own activity (what they like/save/share/comment);
post info incl. **how fast and how many** people like/comment/share/save it (engagement
velocity) + recency + location; the poster's recent interaction strength; the viewer's
history with the poster.
Predicted actions that lift ranking: **dwell time (a few seconds), comment, like, share,
profile-photo tap.**
Do: hook in the first line/frame to win dwell time; write captions that invite comments
and saves; post when the audience is active (velocity matters); give a reason to tap the
profile. Carousels and longer dwell help.

## Stories (closeness with people you follow)
Signals: viewing history, engagement history (likes/DM replies), and **closeness**.
Predicted actions: tap-into-story, reply, vs. skip.
Do: post consistently so regular viewers keep their streak; use reply-driving formats
(question/poll/quiz/emoji-slider stickers, DMs); build closeness (reply, reshare UGC).

## Explore (discovery from accounts you don't follow)
Here **post popularity/engagement velocity matters much MORE than in Feed.**
Signals: post popularity (how fast/many like, comment, **share, save**); the viewer's
Explore activity; their history with the poster; the poster's recent engagement.
Predicted actions: **likes, saves, shares.**
Do: optimise hard for **saves and shares** (save-worthy: tips, lists, references;
share-worthy: relatable, useful, surprising); engineer strong **early** engagement;
pick broadly-discoverable topics, not just in-jokes for existing followers.

## Reels (entertainment + discovery)
Predicted actions (most important): **reshare, watch all the way through, like, tap the
audio page** (a proxy for "this inspired me to make my own").
Signals: viewer activity; history with the poster; reel content (audio track, visuals) +
popularity; poster popularity/engagement.
Do: lead with an entertaining hook to maximise **watch-through + loops**; keep it tight;
use **trending audio**; prompt **reshares**; make it recreatable (drives audio-page taps).

## Recommendation eligibility — what gets SUPPRESSED in Explore/Reels
Reels are made less visible if they are: low-resolution, **watermarked** (e.g. a TikTok
logo), muted, have visible borders, are **majority text**, or have already been posted on
Instagram (reposts). Avoid all of these. Keep the account's recommendation eligibility
clean (Recommendation Guidelines + Community Standards) so content stays surfaceable.

## Cross-cutting priorities (apply to every post we design)
1. **Saves + shares + sends** are the highest-leverage actions for reach — build a save/
   share reason into the post.
2. **First 1–3 seconds / first line** must hook (dwell + watch-through).
3. **Comments** matter for Feed — end with a question or a take worth replying to.
4. **Trending audio** + original, vertical, full-frame, no-watermark assets for Reels.
5. **Consistency** compounds (Stories viewing history, poster strength).
