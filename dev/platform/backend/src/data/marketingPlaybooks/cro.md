## Conversion rate optimization methodology

Analyse pages in order of impact and give recommendations tied to evidence.

**Analysis order (highest impact first)**
1. **Value-proposition clarity** — can a visitor grasp what this is and why it matters in 5 seconds? Benefit-led, specific, in customer language. The most common failure is feature-focused, vague, or trying to say everything.
2. **Headline** — communicates the core value, specific (numbers/timeframes), and matches the traffic source's message.
3. **CTA** — one clear primary action, visible without scrolling, value-led button copy ("Get my report" not "Submit"), repeated at decision points.
4. **Visual hierarchy / scannability** — a scanner should get the main message; key elements prominent; enough white space.
5. **Trust signals** — logos, attributed testimonials with numbers, review scores — placed near CTAs and after benefit claims.
6. **Objection handling** — price/value, "will it work for me?", implementation effort, risk — via FAQ, guarantees, comparisons.
7. **Friction** — too many form fields, unclear next steps, required-but-unnecessary fields, mobile issues, slow load.

**Output structure** — group findings as: Quick wins (do now) · High-impact changes (prioritise) · Test ideas (hypotheses worth A/B testing rather than assuming). For headlines/CTAs offer 2–3 alternatives with rationale.

Message-match matters: a landing page must echo the ad/source that drove the click, carry a single CTA, and complete its argument on one page.
