## Detailed description — ~500 words

Use this in the **About this app** section.

> ### Built for agency-managed Shopify stores
>
> If your marketing is run by an agency using the **October Marketing Intelligence** platform, this app keeps their dashboards and reports in sync with everything happening in your store — orders, customers, products, inventory, themes, and abandoned checkouts — in real time.
>
> One install, one pairing token from your agency, and the connection runs in the background. No spreadsheets to send. No screenshots. No "can you grab last week's numbers?" emails.
>
> ### What gets synced
>
> - **Orders** — every create, update, cancellation, fulfilment and refund
> - **Customers** — new sign-ups and profile updates, with marketing opt-in status
> - **Products** — catalogue changes, price moves, new variants
> - **Inventory** — stock-level changes so your agency knows what's actually in stock when they write campaigns
> - **Themes** — when you publish a redesign or A/B test
> - **Abandoned checkouts** — captured for remarketing automation
>
> ### What October Marketing Intelligence does with it
>
> Your agency uses this live feed to:
>
> - Generate weekly and monthly performance reports automatically
> - Run conversion and attribution analysis across Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn
> - Spot trends and anomalies in your store activity before they become problems
> - Inform ad creative and campaign decisions with real numbers, not estimates
>
> ### Built read-only on purpose
>
> The app **only reads** — it cannot modify your products, change prices, update customers, or write to your theme. The scopes you grant on install (`read_orders`, `read_customers`, `read_products`, `read_inventory`, `read_themes`) are all the access it ever needs.
>
> ### Where your data lives
>
> All forwarded data is stored on October Marketing Intelligence infrastructure in the United Kingdom (London, AWS eu-west-2). Encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest with AES-256. We're GDPR-compliant and handle Shopify's mandatory data-request, customer-redact and shop-redact webhooks automatically.
>
> Your data is **never sold**, **never shared with third parties** outside this flow, and **never used to train AI models**.
>
> ### Pricing
>
> Free. The app is a connector for the October Marketing Intelligence platform — your agency holds the subscription, you get the integration at no cost.
>
> ### Need help?
>
> Email **support@octobercomms.com** or speak to your account manager at the agency. Privacy and data-deletion requests go to **privacy@octobercomms.com**.

## Support page copy

For when you publish `octobercomms.com/support/shopify`. Roughly 150 words.

> # October Marketing Intelligence — Shopify support
>
> The October Marketing Intelligence Shopify app keeps your store data in sync with your marketing agency's reporting dashboard. If something's not working, this page is the fastest way to get it fixed.
>
> ### Common questions
>
> **My agency asked me to install the app — what do I do?** Click their install link, complete the standard Shopify OAuth, and paste the 24-character pairing token they sent you into the app's embedded admin. It'll flip to "Connected" within seconds.
>
> **Is my customer data safe?** Yes. The app is read-only — it cannot modify anything. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in the UK, and never sold or shared outside the flow. See our [Privacy Policy](https://octobercomms.com/privacy-shopify) for the detail.
>
> **How do I disconnect?** Uninstall the app from Shopify → Apps. We'll receive an `app/uninstalled` notification and stop accepting new events immediately. Existing data is deleted within 30 days.
>
> **Need help?** Email [support@octobercomms.com](mailto:support@octobercomms.com) — we typically reply within one business day.
