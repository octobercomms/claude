# FACTS — Where the Year Goes

Every claim on screen or in the voice, with its source and the wording it comes from. The two
sources are the client's own documents, supplied for this work:

- **DOC A** — *Atlanta Design Festival 2027: Direction for the twentieth year*, September 2026, 18 slides + appendix.
- **DOC B** — *A festival convenes. It does not produce.*, prepared for Bernard by Daniel, October 2026, 6 slides.

No third source exists, and the film says so on screen.

## Inferences, marked as such on screen

| Inference | Basis | How it is marked |
|---|---|---|
| The production calendar (which months each event occupies) | The documents say each event takes "months of build" and name festival week as 25 Sept – 3 Oct, but give no month-by-month schedule. The blocks are a schematic reading, not a record. | The calendar carries `SCHEMATIC · MONTHS, NOT DATES` under its heading throughout. |
| That production and selling occupy the same months | Follows from the stated Q1 sponsor window and the stated build burden. It is an argument, not a measurement. | Stated as a finding, in the film's own voice, not quoted as a document line. |
| "The large one is the sponsorship and the grants that were never worked" | DOC B lists grants as "2028 money" and sponsorship as largely unsold; the size of the forgone amount is nowhere measured. | The Unresolved column says plainly that nobody has priced the months and that no figure was measured. |

## Things the film deliberately does not claim

- It does not name which two revenue lines have sold, because DOC B does not.
- It does not put a number on the forgone sponsorship, because neither document does.
- It does not treat any ADF figure as independently verified: both documents are internal, and the
  film says on screen that no third source exists.
