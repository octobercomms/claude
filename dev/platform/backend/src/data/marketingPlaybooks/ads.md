## Paid ads methodology

Goal: efficient customer acquisition. Judge campaigns on the metrics that match their objective, and tie recommendations to the specific lever to pull.

**Metrics by objective** — Awareness: CPM, reach, view rate. Consideration: CTR, CPC. Conversion: CPA, ROAS, conversion rate.

**Optimisation levers**
- *CPA too high*: check the landing page first (is the problem post-click?), then tighten targeting, test new creative angles, improve relevance/quality score, adjust bids — in that order.
- *CTR low*: creative isn't resonating (new hooks/angles), audience mismatch (refine targeting), or ad fatigue (refresh creative).
- *CPM high*: audience too narrow (expand), high competition (new placements), or low relevance (better creative fit).
- *Frequency climbing*: fatigue — refresh creative or widen the audience.

**Budget** — Testing: 70% proven / 30% new. Scaling: consolidate into winners, raise budgets 20–30% at a time, wait 3–5 days between increases for the algorithm to re-learn. Don't judge or kill a campaign before ~50 conversions of data.

**Retargeting** — Segment by funnel stage (cart/trial = hot, key pages = warm, any visit = cold) with tighter windows + higher frequency for hotter audiences. Always exclude existing customers and recent converters — spending on people who already bought is waste.

**Attribution caveat** — platform-reported conversions are inflated and double-count across platforms. Reason in blended terms (blended CAC/ROAS across all spend), compare to GA4, not just in-platform CPA.

**Copy frameworks** — PAS (Problem→Agitate→Solve), BAB (Before→After→Bridge), social-proof lead (stat/testimonial → what you do → CTA).

When recommending action, name the specific campaign/ad set, the lever, and the evidence — never "consider improving creative" in the abstract.
