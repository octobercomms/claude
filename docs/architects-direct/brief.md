# Architects Direct — Project Brief

*Prepared by October Communications | July 2026. Transcribed from the original
`Architects_Direct_Brief.docx`. This brief is a working document; figures marked as
indicative should be agreed before platform build begins.*

## 1. Background

Tiam Architects operates at the premium end of London's residential market, with a
portfolio of complex conservation, heritage and high-spec projects. The practice
generates leads primarily through paid social (Instagram) and press coverage, at a
current cost of approximately £166 per lead.

A second, distinct market exists below that threshold: homeowners who need competent,
straightforward architectural drawings for planning applications, building control
submissions, permitted development or tender packages. These clients are price-sensitive,
process-driven and numerous. Tiam is currently not set up to serve them, and does not
want to serve them under the Tiam brand.

Architects Direct is the proposed solution: a separate, self-service architectural
service operating as a sister company, designed to capture high-volume, lower-complexity
work without compromising the positioning of the core practice.

## 2. Objectives

- Generate revenue from a market segment Tiam currently ignores.
- Run at low overhead: minimal client contact, standardised output, automated where possible.
- Act as a lead feeder: projects exceeding a defined size or complexity threshold are redirected to Tiam.
- Operate independently of the Tiam brand in visual identity, tone and pricing.

## 3. Service Model

Architects Direct offers fixed-price drawing packages for standard residential works.
Pricing is determined by project type and floor area. Clients select and pay online. No
initial call required.

**Package categories:** Planning application drawings · Building control / building
regulations drawings · Permitted development drawings · Tender drawings · Project
coordination (site attendance, consultant liaison).

**Pricing logic** — tiered by floor area, three bands (indicative):

- Up to 50m² — Band A
- 50–100m² — Band B
- 100–150m² — Band C
- Over 150m² or listed building — Price on request / redirect to Tiam

Exact pricing to be set by Tiam before launch, accounting for consultant revenue share
(see Section 5).

**Client flow:**

1. Client lands on Architects Direct website via ad, Bark or search.
2. Selects service type and floor area; receives instant fixed price.
3. Submits project brief via a short self-service form (target: under five minutes).
4. A project account is created automatically. Partial submissions trigger a follow-up email.
5. Tiam completes the drawings and uploads them to the project portal.
6. Client receives a watermarked preview; full drawings released on payment.
7. Payment distributed automatically across Tiam (60%) and any appointed consultants.

## 4. Consultant Structure and Liability

Architects Direct does not employ or directly appoint structural engineers, surveyors or
party wall advisors. Where specialist input is required, consultants are appointed
directly by the client, using terms and conditions embedded in the platform.

Tiam establishes agreements with a preferred panel of consultants who agree to:

- Be responsible for their own scope and professional liability.
- Be paid automatically via the platform on project completion.
- Participate opt-in per project.

Projects requiring structural input below the complexity threshold remain within
Architects Direct. Projects needing ongoing consultant coordination beyond standard scope
are flagged for redirection to Tiam.

## 5. Revenue Model

All revenue collected upfront on delivery (watermark/preview model). Distribution per
project is automatic:

- Tiam: 60%
- Structural engineer (where appointed): 20%
- Surveyor / party wall (where appointed): 20%
- October Communications (traffic and platform): percentage share TBC, deducted from Tiam's portion — no upfront fees.

Consultant splits only apply where that consultant is involved. Simple planning-only jobs
return the full 100% to Tiam minus the October share.

## 6. Brand and Design Direction

- Must read as professional and trustworthy, but **not premium**. Signal functionality and price clarity — similar to Resi's early positioning, or Screwfix: reliable, direct, no ambiguity.
- No Tiam branding, photography or personnel (Bahar's face will not appear in any AD marketing).
- Chunky sans-serif typeface; clear pricing hierarchy; no architectural portfolio imagery.
- Ads generated rapidly at volume; refreshed regularly. Instagram primary channel.
- Bark registered as a secondary lead source for lower-end projects.

## 7. Technical Build

Platform built on WordPress (existing infrastructure — no additional hosting cost).
Phase 1 targets a working version within two weeks.

**Phase 1 — Core:** Service selection and pricing calculator · Self-service intake form
with project account creation · Partial submission follow-up email (automated) · Drawing
upload and watermarked preview delivery · Payment gate before full drawing release.

**Phase 2 — Enhancements:** AI-assisted intake · Automatic revenue distribution to
consultants · Complexity scoring logic (auto-routes oversized/listed projects to Tiam).

## 8. Tiam Redirect Logic

Any project meeting the following is automatically redirected to Tiam for a formal
consultation:

- Floor area exceeds 150m² (or threshold agreed by Tiam).
- Project is a listed building.
- Estimated fee exceeds a defined threshold (to be set by Tiam).
- Client selects "I need ongoing project management" or similar.

This creates a natural funnel: Architects Direct captures volume, Tiam captures the
converted premium leads.
