# Hillcroft Gardens — Terms Templates (DRAFT)

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This is a **starting-point template** drafted to give
> Hillcroft Gardens something concrete to work from. It has **not** been prepared or checked
> by a solicitor. **Before using these terms with any client, have them reviewed by a
> qualified solicitor** (ideally one familiar with UK consumer law and the
> construction/landscaping sector). Laws change and every business is different. Placeholders
> in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]` must be completed by Hillcroft.
>
> These terms assume the client is a **consumer** (an individual, not a business) and that
> Hillcroft operates in **England & Wales**. Key consumer protections referenced: the
> **Consumer Rights Act 2015** and the **Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and
> Additional Charges) Regulations 2013**.

## Document A — Consultation Booking Terms (the £200 consultation)

**1. What you're booking.** A garden design consultation with Hillcroft Gardens at your
property, including a site visit, discussion of your ideas, and initial professional advice.

**2. Fee.** The consultation fee is **£200 (incl. VAT where applicable)**, payable at the time
of booking. **This fee is a separate charge for Donna's time and expertise. It is not a
deposit and is not deducted from the cost of any design or build work you may go on to
commission.**

**3. Rescheduling & cancellation.** You may reschedule or cancel free of charge up to
**[48 hours]** before the appointment. Within that window, the fee is **non-refundable**, as
the time has been reserved for you.

**4. Consumer cancellation right.** As this is a service booked at a distance, you normally
have a **14-day right to cancel** under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. However, if
you ask us to carry out the consultation within that 14-day period and it takes place, you
lose the right to cancel once the service is fully performed. By booking a consultation date
inside the 14-day period you request that we proceed on that basis.

**5. What's not included.** The consultation does not include detailed drawings, renders,
plant schedules, or pricing — these form part of a separate design proposal if you choose to
proceed.

## Document B — Garden Design & Build Terms and Conditions (DRAFT)

### 1. Definitions
- **"We" / "us" / "Hillcroft"** — Hillcroft Gardens, `[legal entity / sole trader name]`,
  `[address]`, `[company no. if applicable]`.
- **"You" / "the Client"** — the person who accepts a Proposal.
- **"Proposal"** — the design, plant schedule, visualisations and itemised pricing we present
  to you (in the online portal and/or as a PDF).
- **"Works"** — the design and/or landscaping works described in the accepted Proposal.

### 2. The contract
A binding contract forms when you **accept the Proposal and electronically sign** these terms
through our client portal (or sign a printed copy), and pay the first payment milestone. The
contract is made up of the accepted Proposal and these terms; if they conflict, the Proposal
prevails for scope and price.

### 3. Quotation validity
Prices in a Proposal are valid for **30 days** from the date issued, after which they may be
revised. Once you accept, the price is **fixed for the agreed scope**, subject to clauses 6
and 7.

### 4. Fees and payment
- Fees and the **payment schedule** are set out in the Proposal. Our standard schedule is
  **50% deposit on signing, 25% on commencement of works, and 25% on completion**, unless the
  Proposal states otherwise.
- Payments are made via the secure payment link in the portal (card payments processed by
  Stripe).
- **The £200 consultation fee is separate and is not credited against these fees.**
- Late payments may incur interest at `[8% above the Bank of England base rate]` under the
  Late Payment of Commercial Debts legislation where applicable, and we may pause Works until
  overdue amounts are paid.

### 5. Deposit
The deposit secures your project slot and funds the ordering of plants and materials. It
becomes **non-refundable once we have ordered materials or scheduled labour for your project**,
save for your statutory cancellation rights (clause 14).

### 6. Site survey and ground conditions
Pricing is based on the information available at the time of the Proposal and on normal ground
conditions. If a **final site survey** or the works themselves reveal conditions we could not
reasonably have foreseen (e.g. rock, made ground, drainage problems, buried obstructions,
contamination, access limitations), we will tell you and agree any necessary **variation** to
scope, price or timescale before proceeding.

### 7. Variations
Any change you request, or that becomes necessary, will be confirmed in writing (a
**variation**) with any adjustment to price and timescale, before that work is carried out.

### 8. Plants, materials and substitutions
- Plants are natural, living products: size, form and colour vary, and **establishment and
  survival are not guaranteed** as they depend on aftercare, weather and conditions.
- Plant and material **availability is seasonal**. Where an item is unavailable we may
  substitute one of **equivalent quality, character and value**, notifying you where the
  change is material.
- Sizes quoted are typical supply sizes at the time of planting, not mature sizes.

### 9. Visualisations and renders
All renders, sketches, watercolours, plans and the seasonal film are **artistic impressions
to illustrate the design intent**. They are **not photographs of the finished garden** and the
completed Works may differ in detail, planting density, colour and finish.

### 10. Intellectual property
All designs, drawings, plant schedules and visualisations remain **our intellectual property**
until paid for in full, after which you receive a **licence to use them for your own garden at
the property only**. They may not be copied, shared for use elsewhere, or used by another
contractor without our written consent.

You will provide **safe access**, and access to **water and electricity**, during the Works,
and confirm the **boundaries** of your property. You are responsible for obtaining any
necessary **permissions** — e.g. planning permission, listed-building or conservation-area
consent, Tree Preservation Order consents, party-wall agreements, and your landlord's or
freeholder's consent. We are not liable for delays or costs arising from missing permissions.

### 12. Timescales
Start and completion dates are **good-faith estimates**. We are not liable for delays caused
by weather, ground conditions, material/plant availability, your delay in providing access or
decisions, or events outside our reasonable control (**force majeure**). We will keep you
informed and agree revised dates.

- If you are a consumer and signed away from our premises, you have a **14-day right to cancel**

- If you ask us to **begin Works within the 14-day period**, you agree we may start, and if you
  then cancel you must pay for work done and materials ordered up to that point.
- After Works begin (or after the cooling-off period), cancellation terms in clause 5 and any
