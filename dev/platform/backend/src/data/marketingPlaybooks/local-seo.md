# Local SEO methodology

Distilled from the MIT-licensed coreyhaines31/marketingskills `seo-audit` and
`ads` skills, focused on local/near-me search and Google Business Profile.

## Local intent & keywords
- **Local searches split into "near me", explicit-city, and implicit-local.**
  "emergency plumber near me", "plumber leeds", and "burst pipe repair" (searched
  from Leeds) all need the same page to rank. Target the city and the service
  together: `[service] [city]`, `[service] near me`, `[urgency] [service] [city]`.
- **Buyer intent ladder.** Immediate-intent modifiers convert best: "emergency",
  "same day", "24 hour", "near me", "open now", "[service] cost/quote". Long-tail
  service+qualifier+city phrases are lower competition and higher conversion than
  head terms — prioritise them.
- **Tag every keyword** with its dominant intent (informational / commercial /
  transactional) and say plainly *why* it converts, so the AM can prioritise.

## Competitive gap thinking
- A competitor outranks because they cover sub-intents you don't: service-area
  pages, FAQs, comparison/"cost" pages, trust content (reviews, accreditations,
  case studies). Name the **specific** missing pages, not "more content".
- Ground every recommendation in what the competitor actually has vs what the
  client has — never generic "write more blogs" advice.

## Google Business Profile (GBP)
- GBP posts should be **local, specific, and action-driven**: lead with a local
  keyword, reference a real landmark/area, give one clear urgency-driven CTA
  ("Call now", "Book today"), keep to ~1500 characters, one idea per post.
- Mine what competitors are *not* posting about (services, seasonal offers,
  questions) — those gaps are the easiest wins.

## Trust & conversion signals (what to surface in a comparison)
Services offered, areas/locations served, unique selling points, and trust
signals (reviews count + rating, certifications/accreditations, guarantees,
years in business, case studies). A side-by-side of these against competitors
reveals the advantages worth pressing.

## Output discipline
Be specific and commercial. British English. Cite the evidence each finding is
based on. No filler, no generic SEO platitudes.
