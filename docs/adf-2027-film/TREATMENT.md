# ADF 2027 — Direction

Swiss Motion Graphics · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · ~105 s · no voice, no subtitles · signal colour `#D8432A`

Source: *Atlanta Design Festival 2027 — Direction for the twentieth year*, September 2026, 18 argument
slides plus a job-description appendix. Audience: Elayne and Bernard. The film is the argument of the
deck, not a summary of it: every figure on screen is from the deck.

---

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A · The sales curve as the spine.**
Open on thirteen selling weeks filling one by one, nine of them producing almost nothing, the last two
slamming. Then why, then the four structural reasons, then the proposal, then the decision. Honest and
linear. Rejected as the whole film: it argues the symptom for sixty seconds and leaves the structure
fighting for the last thirty. The curve is the strongest image in the deck, so it opens the film instead
of carrying it.

**B · Two doors, counted backwards.**
Open on the December deadline, run the clock backwards through the evidence, arrive at A or B. The
countdown is a real constraint (universities run July to June, chapters set budgets in the fall) and
it gives urgency for free. Rejected because the urgency is borrowed, not earned: a deadline is only
frightening once you know what is at stake, and the stake is the structure.

**C · One grid, read four ways.** ← chosen
The frame holds one 12-column grid for the whole film. It is re-read four times, and each re-reading
is the next step of the argument: thirteen weeks of sales → four structural reasons → seventeen
institutions across eight disciplines → twelve months of the year. The same system, told to mean
something different each time, ending split into two doors.

Chosen because the style's subject is *designing programmes*: one system generating many outputs, which
is precisely what the deck proposes. ADF stops producing everything and becomes the grid other people
fill. Form and argument are the same object. It also earns the style's signature move honestly: the
rotation that turns the two-week spike into a twelve-month calendar is the deck's own turn, not a
graphic flourish.

## 2. Logline and arc

> A festival that sells a whole year in two weeks is shown the grid it has been living in, and the
> structure that would let it live in all of it.

**Setup** (0–28 s) Twenty years, and a sales curve where nine weeks of thirteen produce almost nothing.
**Turn** (28–62 s) The problem is not effort. It is that one organisation of two people is the only thing
in the middle. Seventeen institutions snap into the empty modules; the lone red element finally lands on
the grid.
**Ending** (62–105 s) The year opens to twelve months, the shape and the money sit underneath it, and the
frame splits into two legitimate doors with a date on them.

## 3. Benchmark

**Learn from:** Norman McLaren's *Lines Horizontal* for movement that is exact and musical rather than
decorative; Pentagram's identity films for how a type system can carry an argument without a voice;
the BBC *Newsnight* 1980s title sequences for bars that have mass.

**Do not take:** any Müller-Brockmann composition (no concentric circles, no fans), Helvetica or
Akzidenz, any real institution's mark. The seventeen partner organisations appear as named text in the
institutional grid because the deck names them and this film is internal; they appear as set in Archivo,
never as logos.

## 6. Cue map

120 BPM throughout. No tempo change; the pace changes by note density, which is the style's own logic.

| Section | Bars | Instrumentation |
|---|---|---|
| Open | 1–6 | Bass pulse alone, quarter notes. One rim click on 3 |
| Weeks | 7–13 | Add kick on 1 and 3, closed hat on eighths, marimba figure one note per week bar |
| The slam | 13.5–15 | **Everything stops but a single low tone.** The slam is foley, not music |
| Revenue | 16–20 | Kick and bass return, hat on sixteenths, marimba drops to the root |
| Reasons | 20–25 | Four-note marimba cell, one extra voice per reason: the build is the argument |
| Empty | 25–27.5 | **Near-silence.** Bass pulse only, filtered dark |
| Institutions | 27.5–32 | Full kit, marimba doubled, one glockenspiel note per institution |
| Rotation | 32–36 | Band thins to bass and hat so the rotation is audible |
| Shape and money | 36–43 | Kit returns, vibraphone ostinato, bass walks |
| The two doors | 43–48 | Kick drops to half time. Sine glide follows B's border |
| Empty grid | 48–53 | Instruments cut away with the elements. Bass pulse last, then nothing |

Silences: bar 13.5 (the slam) and bars 25–27.5 (the empty grid). Both land before a turn, as DIRECTOR.md §6 asks.

## 8. Title and caption design

No subtitles: the film has no speech. On-screen type is the layout.

- **Giant numerals** Archivo 700, optically flush left, −2 % tracking at ≥ 90 px.
- **Statements** Archivo 600 at 64 px, flush left at a column start, clip-revealed upward.
- **Caption rows** Archivo 500 at 19 px, 1 px rule above, used for every figure's source line.
- **Technical notes** Archivo 400 at 15 px in the lower-right corner: module count, bar counter, section
  number. They are real: the bar counter counts the film's own bars.
- All figures appear as digits, exactly as the deck states them.

## 9. What this is not

Not a sponsor film, not a teaser, not the deck read aloud. Nobody is persuaded by it who was not already
in the room. It ends on a date, because that is what the deck ends on.
