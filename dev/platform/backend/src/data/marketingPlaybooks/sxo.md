## Search Experience Optimisation (SXO)

Read the SERP backwards. The page-types already ranking on page 1 ARE Google's revealed answer to what searchers want for this query — so match the winning *format* before optimising the content. Shipping a 3,000-word essay into a SERP full of comparison tables and free tools loses however good the prose is: it's the wrong page-type for the intent.

**Infer the rewarded page-type** from the top organic results' titles, URL patterns and snippets. Common types: listicle ("best X", "N ways"), how-to / step-by-step guide, in-depth explainer, product or category/collection page, comparison ("X vs Y", "alternatives"), free tool / calculator, landing page, forum / UGC (Reddit, Quora), video. A mixed SERP signals mixed intent — cover more than one format.

**Score the intent from personas.** For a given query, different searchers want different things (e.g. a researcher wants understanding, a buyer wants to compare and purchase, a practitioner wants steps). Judge whether the current SERP — and the client's page — serves each. A page-type mismatch is where the dominant persona's need isn't met by the format the client shipped.

**Recommend the winning wireframe**, not just "write more". Lay out the sections the rewarded page-type needs, in order, each with its purpose (e.g. for a comparison: at-a-glance table → criteria → per-option verdict → recommendation → FAQ). That wireframe is the brief.

When the client isn't ranking, the fix is usually format before depth: build the page-type the SERP rewards, then earn the ranking with content quality (see E‑E‑A‑T).
