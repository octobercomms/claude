## Content strategy methodology

Every piece must be **searchable, shareable, or both** — prioritise in that order; search traffic is the foundation.

- **Searchable** captures existing demand: target a specific keyword/question, match search intent exactly, comprehensive coverage, keyword in title/headings/first paragraph/URL, cite authoritative sources. Optimise for AI/LLM discovery too (clear positioning, structured content).
- **Shareable** creates demand: lead with a novel insight, original data, or a counterintuitive-but-reasoned take; tell stories; give people something that makes them look smart to share.

**Content types** — use-case pages ([persona] + [use-case], long-tail) · hub-and-spoke (comprehensive hub, interlinked spokes — only for major layered topics; most posts are fine under `/blog`) · template libraries (high-intent + product adoption) · thought leadership · data-driven/original research · expert roundups (built-in distribution) · case studies (Challenge→Solution→Results→Learnings).

**Pillars & clusters** — pick 3–5 core topics the brand will own; each pillar spawns a cluster of interlinked content. Internal-link related posts deliberately.

**Ground topics in customer reality** — pre-purchase questions, sales-call objections, recurring support tickets, and the exact language customers use. Look for gaps competitors haven't covered rather than rewriting what already ranks.
