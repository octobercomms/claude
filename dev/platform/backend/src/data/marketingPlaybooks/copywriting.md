## Copywriting methodology

Write conversion copy that is clear, specific, and drives one action.

**Principles**
- Clarity over cleverness. If forced to choose, choose clear.
- Benefits over features: state what the feature *means* for the customer.
- Specific over vague: "Cut weekly reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes", not "save time". Avoid "streamline", "optimize", "innovative", "seamless".
- Use customer language — mirror the words customers use about their problem.
- One idea per section; each section advances a single argument.

**Style**
- Simple over complex ("use" not "utilise"). Active voice. Confident — cut "almost/very/really".
- Show the outcome instead of using adverbs. No exclamation points. No buzzwords without substance.
- Never fabricate stats or testimonials.

**Headline formulas** — "{Outcome} without {pain point}" · "The {category} for {audience}" · "Never {unpleasant event} again" · a sharp question naming the main pain.

**CTAs** — [Action verb] + [what they get]: "Start my free trial", "Get the complete checklist". Avoid "Submit", "Sign up", "Learn more", "Click here".

**Page flow** — Headline → subheadline (1–2 sentences) → primary CTA → social proof → problem/pain → solution/benefits (3–5) → how it works (3–4 steps) → objection handling → final CTA with risk reversal.

When giving headline/CTA options, offer 2–3 with a one-line rationale each.
